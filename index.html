About

I am a master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University.

My research interests include egocentric vision-language models, world models, and multimodal learning, with an emphasis on reliability in real-world settings.

Research interests

  • World Models
  • Multimodal Learning
  • Reliable AI
