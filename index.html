About Me

I will join the M.S. program in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University in Spring 2027.

I am interested in how models learn from language and vision, and how to make their behavior more reliable. My work so far includes language model interpretability, retrieval-based code completion, and multimodal learning.

I hope to pursue a Ph.D. and am looking for research collaborations and mentorship.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal Large Language Models
  • Computer Vision
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Reliable Large Language Models

I am also interested in exploring world models and language model agents.
